Questions.

Does it work with every app?

Demoty records whatever is on your screen. A few apps protect their content, like some video streaming apps, and those show as black, as they do in any screen recording.

Which iPhones does Demoty run on?

Any iPhone with iOS 26 or later.

Where are my recordings stored?

On your iPhone, and in your private iCloud if iCloud Sync is on. You can turn it off in Demoty's Settings.

What does Pro add?

Every iPhone frame beyond the iPhone 17, your own photos as backgrounds, and exports longer than 10 seconds.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Cancel anytime in Settings › your name › Subscriptions, at least a day before it renews. Lifetime is a one-time purchase.
