Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

Demoty is developed by OThmane Mdidet. This policy explains how the app handles your recordings, photos, purchases, and optional iCloud sync.

Your recordings and photos

Demoty records your screen only after you start recording through the iOS recording controls. Imported videos, project edits, thumbnails, and chosen background photos are stored on your device. Exported videos are saved to Photos with your permission. We do not upload this content to developer-operated servers, and we cannot view your recordings.

iCloud sync

When iCloud Sync is enabled and you are signed in to iCloud, Demoty uses Apple CloudKit to store recordings, thumbnails, background photos, and project settings in your private iCloud database. iCloud Sync is enabled by default and can be turned off in Demoty Settings. Apple processes this data under its own privacy terms. The developer does not have access to the contents of your private iCloud database.

Turning off sync stops future syncing; it does not automatically remove content already in iCloud. You can delete projects in Demoty while sync is enabled, or manage app storage in your iCloud settings. Copies you exported or shared are managed separately.

Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions and other in-app purchases through StoreKit. Demoty checks purchase entitlements to unlock Pro. We do not receive your payment card details or billing address. Apple may provide sales and financial reports to the developer.

Permissions and preferences

Demoty uses the system video and photo pickers to access media you select, and requests permission to save exports to Photos. Settings such as appearance, accent color, onboarding, and purchase entitlement status are stored locally. You can change permissions in iOS Settings.

No ads or tracking

Demoty contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across other apps or websites. You do not need to create a Demoty account.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send so we can answer your request. Please avoid sending sensitive screen recordings or other private content unless necessary.

Children

Demoty does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer, please contact us.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated if Demoty’s data practices change. Questions or deletion requests about support correspondence can be sent to mrmdidet@gmail.com.
