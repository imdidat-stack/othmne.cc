Free and Pro features

The free version includes screen recording, the iPhone 17 frame, bundled backgrounds, editing tools, and exports up to 10 seconds. Demoty Pro unlocks additional iPhone frames, your own photo backgrounds, and exports of any length supported by your device.

Subscriptions and lifetime purchase

Pro is offered as a monthly subscription, a three-month subscription, or a one-time lifetime unlock. The purchase screen shows the current price, currency, and billing period for your storefront before you confirm. A lifetime unlock is a one-time purchase, not an auto-renewing subscription.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Apple charges your Apple Account when you confirm a purchase and for renewals according to the purchase terms shown by Apple. Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting Demoty does not cancel a subscription. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, subject to Apple’s policies and your statutory rights.

Restoring purchases and support

Use Restore Purchases in Demoty with the Apple Account used for purchase. For assistance, email mrmdidet@gmail.com.

Availability and privacy

Recording and export capabilities depend on your device, iOS version, storage, and source content. Keep a separate copy of important recordings. See the Privacy Policy for information about local storage and optional iCloud sync.
