Story

I like making the whole thing.

I usually begin with something that feels noisier than it should: checking app numbers, meeting someone new, finding the right wallpaper, or understanding where money went.

Then I sketch, prototype, build, throw away a few versions, and keep going until the product feels obvious. Working independently means the person tuning an icon curve is also the person debugging the last edge case. I like that—it keeps every decision close to the people using the app.

Each project is different, but the goal stays the same: useful software should feel calm, personal, and considered.
